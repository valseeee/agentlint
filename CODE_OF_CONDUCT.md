# Code of conduct

AgentLint contributors are expected to communicate respectfully and focus feedback on code, design and observable behavior.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, threats, publishing private information, deliberate disruption and bad-faith security reports.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when necessary to protect the project and its community. Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers rather than escalating them in public threads.
